The six screens that exist

built.html documents five OBJECTS — the menu row, the road tablet, the hero card, the heading slab, the caption chip. It is still correct about those. What it no longer covers is which SCREENS exist, because five have been built since it was written.

Read its section B&C with that in mind. It is headed "the road, levels two and three, every section", and that is no longer true: the road now carries three sections, and STUDY's level two stopped being a road at all.

Eleven screens, three levels.
LEVEL 1the standing menu — a vertical column of six section rows, one open at a time. Documented in built.html section A.
LEVEL 2six, one per section: STUDY path · DAILY road · READING library · DRILLS road · JLPT ascent · RECORDS ledger
LEVEL 3four: STUDY deck · STUDY feed · DAILY road · DRILLS road

THE FOUR SHAPES

Every screen below is one of four shapes, and the shape follows the DATA rather than the section. Chain — ordered and gated, exactly one item open. Peers — a small unordered set, all available. List — a large flat set, all available, no order. Dashboard — figures you read, not places you go. Choosing the wrong one is the mistake this menu has made twice.

AND THE ROAD, WHICH STILL EXISTS

The carousel from built.html B&C is unchanged and still carries six screens: DAILY and DRILLS at level two and three, and READING at level three until its library goes one level deeper. It is the right shape for a small unordered set — four games you pick between — and the wrong one for an ordered gated run, which is what it was doing at STUDY.
