Depth is the sentence. What is cleared sinks 9px and takes a short shadow; what is ahead stands 6px proud and casts a long one, so behind and ahead stop being mirror images. Distance from the selection scales the stone (1 → 0.78) and fades it (1, 1, 0.92, 0.72, 0.45, then nothing) — only nine stones are ever painted, which is the constraint the long decks break. The thin posts are terminals at 26px wide; a divider is 18px and wears the colour of what it hands over to.
