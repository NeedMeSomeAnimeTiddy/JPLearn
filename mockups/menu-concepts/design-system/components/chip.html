CHIP

One object at four scales. The streak chips along the top, the figure stack beside the course readout, and the keyboard caps in the corner are all the same thing: ink on cream, the −8° skew, a hard offset shadow, and a vermilion figure. Anything that needs to state a number over the world is a chip.

RULES

THE FIGURE IS DISPLAY, THE LABEL IS NOTb is Segoe UI Black italic 14px at zero tracking in vermilion; the words around it are Bahnschrift 600 11px at 0.14em. That contrast is the whole chip.
THE CAP TAKES NO TEXT SHADOWDark ink on a cream slab carrying a black halo doubles every stroke — at 10.5px that is the stroke width again, and it is why ENTER and ESC were once the hardest words on the screen. No chip in this family has one.
THE LEAN ALTERNATES DOWN A STACKrotate(-1.2deg) then rotate(1deg), so a column of chips reads as laid down rather than as a list of identically tilted boxes.
FIX THE WIDTH BEFORE THE NUMBERS MOVEA counting figure changes the chip's width and shoves its neighbours 8px sideways every tick. Measure the widest value the slot will ever hold and set that width; do not let content size a live figure.
