COURSE · SHEET

Sixteen ordered steps as a printed sheet: ink on cream, a vermilion annotation dot on every step behind you, dot leaders out to the number, and the pick a vermilion slab running 70px past the torn edge and ending in a point. The tear is a pseudo-element rather than a clip on the wrap, so the pick can still overhang it.

A block-level <button> still shrinks to its content. display: block does not make it fill — which is why the first build's leaders came out 18px long and every number sat at a different x. The wrap is a flex column: rows stretch for free, and a flex item's negative right margin actually widens it (an over-constrained block box has its margin-right ignored).
