ACTION SLAB

Bottom right of the interface, and the only element that says what pressing Enter will do. It began as a caption describing the highlighted row and became the action itself — three lines: where you are, what it is, and what happens. Its third line is written from the item's own kind, so it can never promise something the item cannot do.

RULES

WHAT IT SAYS IS WHAT IT WILL DOThe third line comes from the item's kind — a deck opens blocks, a hand-off names the section it flies to, and something with no tutor yet says so instead of lying. A slab that reads START on a dead item is the bug this component exists to prevent.
ONE VERMILION FILL PER SCREENWhen the slab is go it is the action, and nothing else may carry a vermilion fill except the current selection — which is the same act, seen in the list.
THE LEAD IS #ffd9a1, NOT CREAMWashi at two-thirds on a vermilion fill is dimmed body text, not a lead. #ffd9a1 is the one warm cream that reads bright on vermilion without going pink.
nowrap, NO overflow: hidden, NO FIXED WIDTH The box is max-content. Clipping was there to protect a fixed width, and all it can do now is cut words off mid-resize — exactly the frame a text swap is trying to hide.
