COLOUR

Four working colours and a night ground. The palette is small on purpose — every one of these has a JOB, and the job is what decides which one a new element gets. Nothing here is chosen for variety.

THE LAW · WHAT EACH COLOUR MEANS

GOLDEarned. Progress that has happened: a finished step, a filled bar, a mastery figure. Never used for something you can press.
VERMILIONThe one thing to press. Exactly one element per screen carries a vermilion fill — the selection, the action. Two is a bug.
INKNot yet, and the ground for everything. A locked step, an empty track, the wash behind type standing over the world.

GOLD#cfa45c

Earned, lit. Figures and the fill of the step you are on.

NIGHT#171c2c

The valley after dark; #10131e deep, #222941 high.

The one warm cream that reads bright on a vermilion fill without going pink.

Each accent ships as three values — base, deep, lit — set on the interface as --sec, --sec-lo, --sec-hi when a section opens. The accent tints the panel and the section's own marks; it never replaces the law above. STUDY's accent being gold and DAILY's being vermilion is a coincidence of place, not a second meaning.
