Backdrops — what each section actually stands on

Every one of these is the real mockup at 1280×720 with the whole interface switched off: the panel, the chrome, the key hints and the darkening scrim. This is the ground a design has to survive, and it is not one ground — the camera stands somewhere different in each section, so a layout that reads beautifully over a sunset valley can disappear over a lit plaza.

Design against the section you are designing for. If a screen has to work everywhere, check it against JLPT and RECORDS — they are the two extremes.

THE SIX

Two things these pictures are for. First: put the matching one behind whatever you are designing, at 1280×720, rather than the generic valley-sunset.png — that plate is RECORDS' view and nobody else's. Second: they show how much of the frame the interface is currently allowed to cover, which is the live question. The valley is the reason this menu exists; a screen that buries it has spent the whole budget of the idea.
