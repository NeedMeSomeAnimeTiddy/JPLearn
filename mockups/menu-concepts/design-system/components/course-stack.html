COURSE · STACK

Sixteen slabs skewed 11°, fanning right as they recede, each stamped with its number on a perforated stub, bleeding 44px off the panel's left padding. No columns, no rule, no bars — the fill IS the state and the stub is the number.

A slab at 28% is not a slab. The first build tinted the finished steps gold at 0.28 and you could read the pavilion's roof straight through them — the top of the stack came out as a murky wash rather than as objects. The fill has to be near-opaque, and what separates a finished slot from an empty one is what is PRINTED on it, not a tint.
