A stack of slabs fans with translateX(calc(var(--i) * 3.4px)), never with a margin. As a margin on a flex item the width comes out of the slab instead, so all sixteen right edges stay in a perfect vertical line and the drift reads as a ragged left margin rather than as a lean. A pure translateX survives the skew unchanged, so the two compose without a wrapper.

THE RULES

SQUARE CORNERS, ALWAYSNo border-radius anywhere, on anything. It is the one standing rule of this UI and the reason the Kickstarter brand tile's 246/1024 radius was dropped when the mark itself was kept.
ONE ANGLE: −8°skewX(-8deg) on everything filled — the word, the back tab, the keycaps, the action slab, every chip. Anything at −6 with a rotation on top is close enough to look like a mistake rather than a decision.
ONE SHADOW: 2px 3px 0 rgba(0,0,0,0.45)Hard, never blurred. Small marks take 2px 2px 0 0.4; a slab lifted over its neighbours takes 4px 5px 0 0.45. Nothing in this project has a soft shadow.
TYPE INSIDE A SKEWED SLAB TAKES NO SKEW OF ITS OWNIt inherits the parent's transform. That is what makes it lie on the slab rather than beside it — set transform: none and let the parent do the work.
